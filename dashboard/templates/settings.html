Methodology & how to extend this

Engine: attack/defence ratios feed a Dixon & Coles (1997) bivariate Poisson model — the same low-score correlation fix (rho) used across most open football-prediction repos — then engine/monte_carlo.py runs a server-side Monte Carlo over all 380 fixtures for title/top-4/relegation probabilities.

Real data: the "Refresh live ratings" button above pulls from the official FPL API. For training a proper fitted model, run scripts/fetch_historical_data.py to pull real historical results via soccerdata (FBref-backed), then feed engine/feature_engineering.py and engine/ml_models.py.

Realistic ceiling: published ensembles (Dixon-Coles + market odds + text signals) have reported ~63% match-result accuracy on the Premier League — treat any single-model claim meaningfully above that with suspicion, including this project's, until scripts/measure_accuracy.py proves otherwise on real results.
