How FPL managers can use this

🎯
Captaincy: Captain Score blends expected goals with win probability — a strong favourite at home usually outranks a small favourite away.
🧤
Defence picks: Clean-sheet odds above come straight from the scoreline grid — handy for GK/defender selection each gameweek.
📅
Transfer timing: Compare a team's home vs away xG across a few gameweeks on the Fixtures page to spot a good run before it's obvious.
🔄
Rotation risk: 9 clubs play in Europe this season — fixture congestion isn't fully modelled in the live odds yet, so double-check midweek fixtures before captaining their forwards.
💎
Differentials: Bring your own ownership % — cross it against Captain Score to find high-upside, low-ownership picks.
⚡
Bench boost: Use the clean-sheet probabilities to identify which bench players might actually return points through defensive bonus points.
